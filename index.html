Our Story

A house built on the bench, not the boardroom.

ABÔ Atelier began as a single workbench and a conviction: that fine jewellery should be made slowly, by people who know the weight of a piece in their own hands before it ever reaches yours.

We are goldsmiths first. Every design starts as a sketch, then a carve, then months of small corrections until a piece earns its place in the collection. We work in limited runs — never mass production — so that each ring, chain, and clasp still carries the mark of the hand that made it.

What began quietly among a small circle of clients has grown into an atelier with the same rule it started with: nothing leaves the workshop until it is ready to be someone's forever piece.

"We don't design for a season. We design for the drawer a piece never leaves — because it's already on your hand." — Founder, ABÔ Atelier
